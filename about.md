This is the development blog for **UTM** — a free, open-source virtualization
and emulation app for macOS, iOS, and iPadOS. It lets you run other operating
systems (Linux, Windows, classic Mac OS, retro consoles, and more) on Apple
hardware, from the M-series Macs all the way down to the iPhone in your pocket.

The app itself lives at [getutm.app](https://getutm.app) and the source code
is on [GitHub](https://github.com/utmapp/UTM). If you'd like to talk about a
post, the [Discussions](https://github.com/utmapp/UTM/discussions) tab is the
best place.
